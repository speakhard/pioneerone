Everything below exists. It is being found, checked and cataloged before any of it goes up.

Pioneer One was documented obsessively while it was being made, largely because documenting it was one of the few things that cost nothing. Thirty-two behind-the-scenes video blogs went out during production. Alongside them there are scripts at several drafts, stills, artwork, cut and alternate footage, commentary tracks recorded with the cast, contemporary press, and the ordinary paperwork of a production run out of two apartments.

There is also the decade after: successive drafts, treatments and pitch material from the versions that were developed and never made. That material is more interesting than it sounds, and more complicated to publish.

Putting it online properly means cataloging it, confirming what may be shared, and presenting it so it is worth reading rather than merely available. That is a real project and it is not this one. This site went up first so that the season itself would be watchable again.
